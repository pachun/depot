Perch support

Perch is an Apple TV app for Aviary media servers. It needs a server to sign in to: your own, or one a friend or family member runs and has given you an account on.

Signing in

  • Enter the server's address exactly as its owner gave it to you, then your username and password for that server.
  • If Perch says it can't reach the server, check the address and that your Apple TV is online. If both are fine, the server may be down; ask its owner.
  • Accounts and passwords are managed by the server's owner, not by Perch.

Playback

  • If picture and sound drift apart, restart the Apple TV.
  • Subtitles can be turned on by default under Settings, and changed during playback.
  • If a title won't start, try another one. If nothing plays, the server is the likely cause.

What you see depends on the server

Each server decides what it offers. Some can add new titles on request and show trailers and ratings; others play only what is already on them.

Running your own server

Aviary is open source: github.com/pachun/aviary.
