Perch privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Perch is an Apple TV app that plays video from an Aviary media server that you or someone you know runs. This policy covers the Perch app.

What Perch collects

Nothing. Perch has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and its developer runs no service that receives information about you or what you watch.

What stays on your Apple TV

  • The address of the server you signed in to, your username on it and a sign-in token, kept in the device's secure storage. Your password is not stored.
  • Artwork the app has already loaded, cached so screens open quickly.

Signing out or deleting the app removes them.

What Perch sends, and to whom

  • Your server. Perch sends your sign-in, what you browse and search for, and your playback position to the server you chose. That server belongs to whoever runs it, and they decide what it keeps. Perch's developer has no access to it.
  • Hosts your server points to. Some video and artwork loads directly from other hosts when your server provides their addresses. Those hosts see your IP address, as any website does.
  • App updates. Perch checks Expo's update service for a newer version of itself. That request carries the app's version and platform and, like any request, your IP address. It carries nothing about your account or viewing.

Children

Perch is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
